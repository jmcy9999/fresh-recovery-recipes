Sweetcorn & Rice Salad

← All recipes
Time: 15–20 min • Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice (cooled)
  • 1 cup sweetcorn
  • Spring onion greens, thinly sliced (tops only)
  • Parsley, chopped
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • Pinch of salt

Instructions

  • Toss rice with sweetcorn, spring onion greens, parsley, oil, lemon, and salt.
  • Chill or serve at room temperature.
