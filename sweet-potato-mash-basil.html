Sweet Potato Mash with Basil

← All recipes
Time: 25 min • Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • 2 medium sweet potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 1–2 tbsp Greek yoghurt
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • Few basil leaves, finely chopped
  • Pinch of salt
  • Optional: drizzle of honey

Instructions

  • Boil sweet potato until very tender (12–15 min).
  • Drain; mash with yoghurt and olive oil.
  • Fold in basil and adjust salt.
