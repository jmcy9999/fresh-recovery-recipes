Cucumber–Tomato Salad with Lemon Quinoa

← All recipes
Time: 25 min • Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • ¾ cup quinoa, rinsed
  • 1½ cups water
  • 1 cup diced cucumber
  • 1 cup diced cherry tomatoes
  • Parsley or basil, chopped
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • Pinch of salt

Instructions

  • Cook quinoa 15 min; fluff and cool slightly.
  • Fold in veg, herbs, oil, lemon, and salt.
