Gentle Lemon Rice

← All recipes
Time: 20 min • Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • ¾ cup jasmine or basmati rice
  • 1½ cups water
  • 1 tbsp olive oil or small knob unsalted butter
  • Zest of 1 lemon
  • Juice of ½ lemon (to taste)
  • Small handful parsley or basil, finely chopped
  • Pinch of iodised salt
  • Optional: carrot matchsticks or sweetcorn

Instructions

  • Rinse rice until water runs clear.
  • Simmer rice with water, covered, 10–12 minutes until tender.
  • Warm oil/butter gently; stir in lemon zest 10–15 seconds.
  • Fluff rice; fold in zest oil, lemon juice, herbs, and salt.
