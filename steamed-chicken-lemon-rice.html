Steamed Chicken with Lemon Rice

← All recipes
Time: 30 min • Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • 2 small chicken breasts
  • 1 batch Gentle Lemon Rice
  • Lemon wedges
  • Chopped parsley
  • Pinch of salt

Instructions

  • Lightly season chicken with salt; steam over gently simmering water 12–15 min until cooked through.
  • Rest 3 min, then slice or shred.
  • Serve over Gentle Lemon Rice with a squeeze of lemon and parsley.
