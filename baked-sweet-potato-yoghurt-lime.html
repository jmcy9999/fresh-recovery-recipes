Baked Sweet Potato with Yoghurt & Lime

← All recipes
Time: 45–60 min • Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • 2 medium sweet potatoes
  • ½ cup Greek yoghurt
  • Lime zest and juice (to taste)
  • Pinch of salt
  • Optional: drizzle of honey

Instructions

  • Bake whole sweet potatoes at 200°C for 40–55 min until very soft.
  • Split, fluff with a fork; top with yoghurt, lime, and a little salt.
